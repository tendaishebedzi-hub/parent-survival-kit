Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Parent Survival Kit (a project of Kugona.ai) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site or buy one of our digital guides, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to align with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

Parent Survival Kit is a small digital-products business based in South Africa. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights below, contact us at kugonacv@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, which you provide at checkout so your guide can be delivered to you.
  • Order details — which guide or bundle you bought and when.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends automatically (such as device and general location), used only to keep the site working and secure.

We do not collect or store your card or banking details. Payment information is handled entirely by our payment processors (see below).

How your purchase is processed

Checkout and delivery of our guides are handled by Payhip. Payments in South African Rand are processed by Paystack; international payments may be processed in US Dollars. These providers receive only the information needed to complete your order, and each has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read:

  • Payhip — payhip.com/privacy
  • Paystack — paystack.com/privacy

How we use your information

  • To deliver the guide you purchased and provide support if you have a problem with your download.
  • To keep records of sales as required for accounting and tax.
  • To protect the site against fraud and technical abuse.

We do not sell your information. We will only send you marketing email if you have separately agreed to receive it, and you can opt out at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to support you and to meet legal and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct it if it is wrong, or delete it where we are not required to keep it. To make a request, email kugonacv@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Children

Our guides are written for parents and are sold to adults. This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Email kugonacv@gmail.com and we’ll help.
